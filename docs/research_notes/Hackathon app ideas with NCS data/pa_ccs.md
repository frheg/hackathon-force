# P&A and CO2 storage (CCS) on the NCS as million-dollar problems for a data/AI app

Research date: 2026-11-17. Currency and dates noted per item. Several primary PDFs (Havtil 2024 TA-wells report, Sodir HTML pages, Wiley/ScienceDirect papers) returned HTTP 403 or were not text-extractable, so some figures below come from search-result snippets of those pages; this is flagged where it applies.

## 1. P&A scale on the NCS: number of wells, total cost, cost per well, rig time, who pays

### Takeaway
About 2,900 NCS wellbores still have to be permanently plugged (Sodir, Nov 2025). Single operations range from about NOK 50 million to over NOK 1 billion per well, and the Norwegian state carries 78% of the cost through the tax system. Older industry totals of NOK 420-900 billion (2013-2016 era) and a 2024 SINTEF figure of "upwards of NOK 800 billion" are the only total-cost numbers found. No newer official total was found.

### Cited Findings
- Sodir (13 Nov 2025, "New technology is profitable when shutting down wells"): about 2,900 wellbores on the NCS are yet to be plugged; the simplest plugging operations have cost about NOK 50 million and complex ones more than NOK 1 billion; 30-40 wells are plugged per year while 100-150 new production wells are drilled per year; 78% of P&A cost is covered by the state (which also receives 78% of petroleum revenues); about half of current production comes from wells drilled in the last five years. - [Sodir news via energy-pedia mirror](https://www.energy-pedia.com/news/norway/new-technology-is-profitable-when-shutting-down-wells-201921); original: [Sodir](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2025/new-technology-is-profitable-when-shutting-down-wells/) (403 on direct fetch)

- Sodir decommissioning cost reporting also exists in the 2017 resource report. - [Sodir Resource report 2017, decommissioning costs](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2017/cessation/decommissioning-costs/) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- Order of magnitude: at ~2,900 wellbores and a per-well range of NOK 50 million to 1 billion+, the remaining NCS P&A bill is plausibly several hundred billion NOK (roughly USD 40-80 billion at ~NOK 10-11/USD). The state's 78% share means public money is the dominant payer, which makes "cost per well saved" a strong pitch to Sodir/Havtil-type judges.
- Even a 1 rig-day saving per well at a NOK 4 million/day spread (old rate; current rates likely higher) across ~2,900 wells is ~NOK 11.6 billion. A 2-day app cannot prove such savings, but this arithmetic is a defensible "million-dollar framing".
- The mismatch (100-150 wells drilled vs 30-40 plugged per year) means the backlog is growing; the 488 TA/inactive wells and rising red/orange count are a concrete, regulator-visible pain point.

### Gaps
- No current (2024-2026) official NOK total for all remaining NCS P&A was found; the Offshore Norge/Havtil 2025 10-year cost forecast (2024-2033, NOK 2024) exists but figures were unreadable in snippets.
- Current rig day rates for P&A on the NCS were not found.
- Havtil 2024 TA-wells PDF could not be text-extracted (no poppler on host); details on documentation quality were not obtained.

## 2. Regulation (NORSOK D-010) and what data is needed to plan P&A

### Takeaway
NORSOK D-010 Rev 5 (2021) governs NCS P&A, with Element Acceptance Criteria (EAC) tables for casing cement, cement plugs, creeping formation (shale as barrier, EAC table 52), and perf-wash-cement. Planning needs per-well casing/cement records, cement logs, formation tops and caprock/creeping-shale intervals: much of this lives in legacy PDF/TIFF reports, which is the data-extraction opportunity.

### Cited Findings
- D-010 Rev 5 (2021) significantly reworked the P&A chapter: changed cement height adjustments and EAC tables for casing cement, cement plug, creeping formation and material plug; added tables for perf/wash/cement and for cement plugs in section-milled intervals. - [Offshore Norge, NORSOK D-010 status new revision (Thorsrud/Holmvassdal)](https://www.offshorenorge.no/contentassets/4cde29b0013b4126b1822d5ba5a5e3d4/kl-0930---0950-arild-thorsrud--ole-petter-holmvassdal-norsok---norsok-d-010-status-new-revision.pdf); [SPE, Updated NORSOK Standard D-010 (2024)](https://file.streaming.spe.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/22133740/Updated-NORSOK-Standard-D-010.pdf)
- Permanent abandonment requirements for creeping formations (shale barriers) are in EAC table 52 of Rev 5/2021; Havtil published a knowledge-gathering report on creeping formation in Nov 2025. - [Havtil, knowledge gathering creeping formation (25 Nov 2025)](https://www.havtil.no/contentassets/2731f45e32c8435abd09809d794fc3b7/knowledge-gathering-creeping-formation_final-25112025.pdf)
- Perf-wash-cement (PWC) is qualified as an alternative to section milling for establishing a verifiable well barrier per D-010. - [Havtil PwC qualification report](https://www.havtil.no/contentassets/6337cbe4b51a4eb48255652616cb728e/pwc-qualification-final-signert.pdf)
- Havtil technology report on permanent plugging methods on the NCS (2021). - [Havtil, Technology and methods for permanent plugging on the NCS](https://www.havtil.no/en/explore-technical-subjects2/technical-competence/reports-from-projects/2021/technology-and-methods-for-permanent-plugging-on-the-ncs/)
- Operators list barrier evaluation, overburden barriers and dual-string logging as top technical needs (2023). - [Offshore Norge P&A outlook 2023-2028](https://www.offshorenorge.no/contentassets/de2f08a3fd4f4796bc00d05dde457e34/plug-and-abadonment-outlook-ncs-2023---2028.pdf)
- Sodir has converted about 92,000 well documents from PDF to AI-readable files (JSON), as part of converting several hundred thousand PDF and TIFF reports, logs and documents. - [Sodir 2026, Large volumes of data ready for AI](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2026/large-volumes-of-data-from-the-norwegian-offshore-directorate-ready-for-ai) (from snippet; page 403 on fetch)
- Structured wellbore data (formation tops, cores, LOT/FIT, DST, mud, coordinates) from Sodir FactPages is standardized on Zenodo (Open Well Data 1.5.0; e.g. ~9,876 wellbore coordinate rows, ~36,752 mud rows; updated 16 Nov 2026). - [Zenodo Open Well Data FactPages tables](https://zenodo.org/records/24097890); [Zenodo formation tops/cores/LOT/DST](https://zenodo.org/records/24270272); [FactPages wellbore attributes](https://factpages.sodir.no/en/wellbore/attributes)
- A peer-reviewed open dataset on P&A status of Troll exploration wells exists. - [Data in Brief / PMC, Troll P&A status dataset](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8181855/)
- A Norwegian open-source P&A database was proposed (c. 2016). - [ResearchGate, Development of a Norwegian Open-Source P&A Database](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/312050775_Development_of_a_Norwegian_Open-Source_Plug-and-Abandonment_Database_With_Applications)

### Inferences
- The minimum data model for an automated P&A pre-planner: wellbore header (FactPages), formation tops (FactPages), casing program and cement tops/volumes (final well reports / completion reports, mostly unstructured), cement log intervals (log files, sometimes only images), and candidate barrier formations (caprock, creeping shale such as Lower Tertiary or Jurassic shales). Only the first two are already structured; the rest is the LLM extraction target.
- Sodir's 92k JSON-converted documents plus FactPages give a hackathon team an unusually good starting corpus for "read the well reports, emit a draft well barrier schematic and candidate barrier depths, with citations".

### Gaps
- Exact D-010 numeric criteria (e.g. minimum plug lengths, cement height in annulus, shale barrier qualification log criteria) were not retrieved; the standard is paywalled (Standard Norge).
- No source quantified how many engineer-hours are spent gathering legacy well data per P&A well on the NCS.

## 3. ML/LLM approaches for P&A (2020-2026)

### Takeaway
ML for cement-log interpretation is well studied (2020-2024) but accuracy versus expert labels is modest (e.g. 51.6% exact match on bond quality in one 25-well study). LLM/GenAI tools for building well barrier schematics from legacy documents exist commercially (SLB Wellbarrier GenAI, 2026) with human review, but no peer-reviewed accuracy benchmarks were found. Campaign optimisation via ML was not found in NCS literature in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Deep neural networks for cement evaluation logs (25 wells): bond quality calls exactly matched reference interpretations 51.6% of the time. - [ResearchGate, Automatic interpretation of cement evaluation logs (2020)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/342630351_Automatic_interpretation_of_cement_evaluation_logs_from_cased_boreholes_using_supervised_deep_neural_networks)
- Feature engineering combining CBL and acoustic impedance features gives efficient models. - [ResearchGate, Better automatic interpretation through feature engineering (2021)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349896210_Better_Automatic_Interpretation_of_Cement_Evaluation_Logs_through_Feature_Engineering)
- ADNOC Onshore used ML to interpret multi-vendor cement bond logs, arguing visual interpretation is subjective. - [ResearchGate, Assisted Cement Log Interpretation Using ML](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/365834333_Assisted_Cement_Log_Interpretation_Using_Machine_Learning)
- ML-assisted cement integrity evaluation for P&A (SPE-216950-MS, ADIPEC 2023); Ouronova + Repsol Sinopec Brazil "P&A Assistant" interprets acoustic logs and supports campaign planning. - [ResearchGate, SPE-216950-MS](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/374382986_Machine_Learning_Assisted_Cement_Integrity_Evaluation_During_Plugging_and_Abandonment_Operations)
- AI-based cement bond quality assessment as "a first step for optimizing P&A design" (2024). - [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/380236513_AI-Based_Cement_Bond_Quality_Assessment_A_First_Step_for_Optimizing_PA_Design)
- Supervised ML for cement integrity (SPE Drilling Conference 2024). - [OnePetro](https://onepetro.org/SPEDC/proceedings-abstract/24DC/24DC/D022S004R001/542936)
- Through-tubing logging + ML for cement integrity (2023); transfer learning on VDL images (2024). - [ScienceDirect through-tubing](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2949891023004694); [ScienceDirect transfer learning VDL](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2949891024003300)
- SLB (2026) markets Wellbarrier with GenAI chaining OCR, NLP and LLMs to turn scanned well reports into structured data and schematics, claiming prep goes "from hours of manual work to largely automated"; its API blog says a human must review output. Vendor claims, not independently verified. - [SLB article 2026](https://www.slb.com/resource-library/article/2026/transforming-plug-and-abandonment-with-wellbarrier--and-generative-ai); [SLB blog, from data ingestion to well barrier schematics](https://www.slb.com/resource-library/blogs/di/revolutionizing-the-well-barrier-life-cycle)
- IPT Global SureTec WellSchematic: cloud barrier diagrams, auto-generation from operational data. - [IPT Global](https://iptglobal.com/tag/wellschematic/)
- Open-source code: a GitHub PR adds permanent P&A barrier screening and a barrier graph for leak-path ranking (no document extraction). - [GitHub digitalmodel PR #3822](https://github.com/vamseeachanta/digitalmodel/pull/3822)

### Inferences
- An LLM pipeline that ingests Sodir's JSON well documents and FactPages and outputs (a) a draft D-010-style barrier schematic, (b) candidate shale/caprock barrier intervals from formation tops, (c) a data-gap checklist per well, with page-level citations, is buildable in 2 days and maps directly onto what SLB sells commercially.
- Cement-log ML is a weaker 2-day target: training data (labelled CBL/USIT) is not openly available for NCS, and published accuracy is modest.
- Campaign batching (grouping wells by field, rig type, complexity class) can be framed as a simple optimisation over an LLM-extracted complexity score; no NCS ML study found, so this is novel but unvalidated.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed benchmark of LLM extraction of casing/cement data from well reports was found.
- No quantified NCS savings from campaign batching found in this pass (Equinor "industrialising plugging" implies it, but no number).

## 4. CCS on the NCS: licences, capacities, status

### Takeaway
By end-2025 Sodir counted 13 CO2 storage licences (1 exploitation, 12 exploration), with Northern Lights (Aurora) injecting since 2025 at 1.5 Mtpa (Phase 2 to at least 5 Mtpa). Sodir's atlases estimate about 70 Gt theoretical capacity in the North Sea and 5.5 Gt in the Norwegian Sea. Snøhvit's Tubåen pressure problem (2011) is the key cautionary lesson on injectivity/capacity.

### Cited Findings
- Sodir "The Shelf in 2025": three CO2 storage licences awarded in 2025; 13 in total (1 exploitation, 12 exploration); Northern Lights completed first CO2 injection in 2025. - [Sodir, New industries on the shelf (2025)](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/the-shelf/the-shelf-in-2025/new-industries-on-the-shelf/) (from snippet; 403 on fetch)
- Northern Lights Phase 1: up to 1.5 Mtpa; Phase 2 raises total to at least 5 Mtpa. - [Equinor, Northern Lights](https://www.equinor.com/energy/northern-lights)
- Northern Lights injection permit (Miljødirektoratet, 30 Apr 2025): 4 of 5 relevant legacy P&A wells have effective primary and secondary barriers; the fifth has one barrier but is unlikely to be reached by the plume even at 180 Mt injected; no preventive well intervention needed. - [Offshore Norge, FORCE legacy wells seminar 19 Nov 2025 (slide 14)](https://www.sodir.no/4add7a/globalassets/2-force/2025/legacy-wells/offshore-norge---when-will-legacy-well-leakage-risk-in-co2-storage-sites-require-well-intervention.pdf)
- Smeaheia (Equinor): licences Albondigas and Kinno each expected around 5 Mtpa; ambition up to 20 Mtpa. - [Equinor, Smeaheia](https://www.equinor.com/energy/smeaheia)
- Luna: 120 km off Bergen, 5 Mtpa, in planning, 2030 start; Poseidon: potentially more than 5 Mtpa (IOGP map, July 2025). - [IOGP Europe CO2 storage projects map July 2025](https://iogpeurope.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CO2-Storage-Projects-in-Europe-map_July25.pdf)
- Errai (Neptune/Horisont): 4-8 Mtpa target per 2023-era reports; current status not verified. - [Hydrocarbon Processing factbox 2023](https://hydrocarbonprocessing.com/news/2023/03/carbon-storage-projects-across-europe)
- Sodir atlas: North Sea about 70 Gt (2011 atlas); Norwegian Sea 5.5 Gt, of which mature areas only 0.17 Gt. 2014 summary chapter lists e.g. Utsira and Skade Formations 15.8 Gt, a Troll aquifer 14 Gt, North Sea producing fields by 2050 10 Gt (table text jumbled in snippet). - [Sodir CO2 atlases](https://sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/co2-atlases/); [Sodir atlas summary ch. 7](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/co2-atlases/co2-atlas-for-the-norwegian-continental-shelf/7-summary-storage-capacities-of-the-norwegian-continental-shelf); [Norwegian Sea atlas](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/co2-atlases/co2-storage-atlas-of-the-norwegian-sea); [Barents Sea atlas PDF](https://www.sodir.no/490769/globalassets/1-sodir/publikasjoner/atlas-eng/co2-atlas-barents-sea.pdf)
- Sleipner: 18.6 Mt stored by end-2019; Snøhvit mid-case 24 Mt (2014 atlas) but Tubåen operations ceased due to rapid unexpected pressure build-up. - [OGCI CO2 Storage Resource Catalogue Cycle 3, Appendix C (2022)](https://www.ogci.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/CSRC_Cycle_3_Appendix_C_Europe.pdf)
- Snøhvit: CO2 injected into Tubåen until April 2011; pressure rose faster than expected; injection moved to Stø Formation. ### Inferences
- Combined planned NCS injection (Northern Lights 5+, Smeaheia up to 20, Luna 5, Poseidon 5+, Errai 4-8 Mtpa) is tens of Mtpa; every licence must assess legacy wells and injectivity, so a screening tool has a clear, growing user base (13 licences and rising).
- Snøhvit is the standard example of why early screening of reservoir quality/injectivity from well data matters: a misjudged reservoir forced re-completion.

### Gaps
- Current licence list with operators, award dates and blocks was not retrieved (Sodir pages 403). The FactPages likely hold this.
- No reliable national total capacity figure in Gt (atlas tables mixed theoretical and effective capacities).

## 5. Legacy wells as CCS leakage risk: screening criteria and cost of reassessment

### Takeaway
Legacy wells are typically the main uncertainty in CCS containment risk; Norwegian CO2 regulations require risk assessment of all relevant legacy wells using available data on their status. Different published screening methods give systematically different classifications for the same NCS wells, and no machine-readable P&A database exists. Remediation is very expensive (UK estimate GBP 25-30 million, about 2 months rig time per intervention), so the industry argues for risk-based acceptance rather than re-entry.

### Cited Findings
- Emmel et al. (2025, GHG Sci & Tech): compared three screening methods on legacy wells in two offshore western Norway storage licences; different methods produce systematically different classifications; no standardised machine-readable P&A records database exists. - [Wiley, A Comparison of Screening Methods for Assessing Legacy Wells Within CO2 Storage Licences: Offshore Norway](https://scijournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ghg.70045?af=R) (from snippet; 403 on fetch)
- Screening of NCS petroleum reservoirs for CO2 storage from public field and well data, using storage capacity per legacy well as a leakage-risk proxy (2026). - [ScienceDirect, Screening CO2 storage potential of petroleum reservoirs on the NCS](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1750583626000411) (from snippet; 403 on fetch)
- Norwegian CO2 safety regulation requires risk assessment of relevant legacy wells within and outside the storage complex based on available data on the status and condition of all wells (DNV, FORCE seminar 19-20 Nov 2025). - [DNV, Legacy-well assessment through best practice](https://www.sodir.no/4add82/globalassets/2-force/2025/legacy-wells/dnv---legacy-well-assessment-through-the-lens-of-best-pratice-guidelines-and-standards.pdf); [FORCE CCS Legacy Wells Seminar page](https://www.sodir.no/en/force/news/2025/in-person-ccs-legacy-wells-seminar)
- Offshore Norge (FORCE, 19 Nov 2025): measures to improve integrity of P&A wells "can be challenging and involve very high costs"; CCS is low-margin; seabed CO2 leakage gives pH impact only within a few tens of metres and CO2 dissolves within 1-2 m above seabed; intervention only if impacts low, success likely and cost not disproportionate. DNV example cited: 3 wells, 3 Mtpa, 25 years, 1% leak likelihood per well, <500 t per leak. - [Offshore Norge FORCE slides](https://www.sodir.no/4add7a/globalassets/2-force/2025/legacy-wells/offshore-norge---when-will-legacy-well-leakage-risk-in-co2-storage-sites-require-well-intervention.pdf)
- Equinor (FORCE 2024): script-based toolbox to estimate CO2 leakage through legacy wells during early screening. - [Equinor, Bello Palacios, FORCE 2024](https://www.sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2024/02.06.-07.-making-good-decisions/presentations/2-4-equinor---alejandro-bello-palacios---co2.pdf)
- SINTEF LEGACY project: tools for screening, modelling, monitoring and mitigation of legacy wells. - [SINTEF LEGACY, FORCE 2025](https://www.sodir.no/4add76/globalassets/2-force/2025/legacy-wells/sintef---legacy-project-_-mapping-monitoring-leakage-modelling-mitigation-of-legacy-wells.pdf); Rose quantitative risking: [Rose, FORCE 2025](https://www.sodir.no/4add6b/globalassets/2-force/2025/legacy-wells/rose-subsurface-assessment---quantitative-risking-of-ccs-projects-_-projects-with-legacy-wells.pdf)
- UK Southern North Sea study: risk assessment of 2,284 wells into four categories; 79% of highest-risk (category 4) wells are classed as Abandoned Phase 3 by NSTA; well age is effectively double-weighted. - [ScienceDirect 2025/26, SNS legacy well risk](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666759225000964)
- Scottish North Sea MCDA study (2025): legacy wells are often the main source of uncertainty; NRAP-Open-IAM is time-intensive across thousands of wellbores. - [IJGGC 2025, MCDA legacy wellbores](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijggc.2025.104336)
- Remediation cost: UK government assessment, about 2 months on location at about GBP 25-30 million per intervention; ZEP 2019 worst-case EUR 166 million incl. ETS, probability-weighted under EUR 1 million per project. Older reports. - [UK Gov, CO2 Storage Liabilities in the North Sea](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a7c6b8940f0b626628abee6/CO2_Storage_Liabilities_in_the_North_Sea_-_An_Assessment_of_Risks_and_Fi....pdf); [ZEP 2019, CO2 Storage Safety in the North Sea](https://zeroemissionsplatform.eu/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/ZEP-report-CO2-Storage-Safety-in-the-North-Sea.pdf)
- GHGT-17 modelling: median leakage 0.1% of injected CO2 under high abandoned-well density. - [OSTI, GHGT-17](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/3005344)

### Inferences
- The problem that is both expensive and data-limited: each storage licence must classify tens to hundreds of legacy wells from scattered reports; methods disagree; a wrong "needs intervention" call costs on the order of GBP 25-30 million (USD 30-40 million) per well. A transparent, citation-backed, multi-method legacy-well screener over FactPages + well reports directly targets this.
- An app that runs the same wells through several published screening schemes and shows where they disagree (and which missing data drives the disagreement) is novel per Emmel et al. and buildable in 2 days.

### Gaps
- Number of legacy wells per NCS CO2 licence (beyond Northern Lights' 5 relevant wells) not found.
- NCS-specific (NOK) cost of legacy-well reassessment studies not found.
