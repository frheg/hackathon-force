Research date: 2026-11-17. Note on method: sodir.no returns HTTP 403 to automated fetches, so Sodir figures below come from search-engine extracts of Sodir pages (URL cited) or from norskpetroleum.no (the official Ministry/Sodir portal), which could be fetched in full. ## 1. Production: forecasting bias, deferred production, optimisation value

### Takeaway
The strongest NCS-specific, quantified problem is forecast bias: on 71 NCS oil fields, operators' FID-time production forecasts over-predicted early production by about 20% (first 5 years), roughly 70% of fields under-delivered, and a trivial "outside view" reference-class forecast beat operators' expensive forecasts. On the optimisation side, the best NCS numbers are Equinor's NOK 2 billion+ from digital solutions in Johan Sverdrup's first year and USD 130 million AI value in 2025; a generic NCS "value of 1% uptime" figure was not found published and must be derived.

### Cited Findings
**Forecast bias (Sodir/FORCE, UiS)**
- Nesvold and Bratvold, "Debiasing probabilistic oil production forecasts", Energy 258 (2022) 124744 (open access, CC BY), hosted by Sodir for the FORCE 2023 seminar. Data: 71 NCS oil fields with first production 1995-2020, using field-level RNB (Revised National Budget) forecasts made available by the NPD/Sodir under anonymity; study sponsored by Equinor. - [Sodir FORCE PDF](https://www.sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2023/dokumenter/debiasing-production-forecasts_published-version.pdf)
- Over the first five production years the shortfall vs. the mean forecast is "around 20%"; after production year 4, "almost 70% of the fields have delivered less oil than expected"; consistent with Nandurdikar and Wallace (2011) attainment of about 80% after PY4. - [same PDF](https://www.sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2023/dokumenter/debiasing-production-forecasts_published-version.pdf)
- Overconfidence: the share of outcomes below the P10 (low) forecast is almost 60% after PY2 and about 40% after PY4 (should be 10%); fewer than ~30% of outcomes after PY2, and about 50% between PY4 and PY10, fall inside the F10-F90 band (should be 80%); fields below F10 never fall below 25% in the first 10 years. - [same PDF](https://www.sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2023/dokumenter/debiasing-production-forecasts_published-version.pdf)
- Valuation impact: with an 8% discount rate, the median discounted produced volume over the first four years is below 3/4 of the discounted mean forecast, and the operators' F10 (low case) "looks like an unbiased forecast". Production start delays were excluded, so true bias is larger. - [same PDF](https://www.sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2023/dokumenter/debiasing-production-forecasts_published-version.pdf)
- Method finding: a reference-class forecast built only from abandoned NCS fields' production curves (low/high = 0.4x/1.6x of reference mean) "by far outperforms the extremely resource-demanding forecasting methods used in industry"; operator forecasts have negative skill score vs. this zero-skill reference; simple multiplicative calibration of operator forecasts is only slightly better than the reference. Results hold across field sizes and over time (train on 1995-2013, test on 2014-2020). - [same PDF](https://www.sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2023/dokumenter/debiasing-production-forecasts_published-version.pdf)
- FORCE 2023 seminar "Curbing optimism and overconfidence bias in forecasting" and companion talks "Field features do not explain greenfield production forecasting bias" and "Production forecasting: optimistic and overconfident, over and over again"; FORCE 2024 "Making good decisions" (Marc Bond, bias and uncertainty). - [FORCE seminar page](https://www.sodir.no/force/Previous-events/2023/in-person-seminar-npdcurbing-optimism-and-overconfidence-bias-in-forecasting); [field features PDF](https://sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2023/dokumenter/field-features-do-not-explain-greenfield-production-forecasting-bias.pdf); [over and over again PDF](https://sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2023/dokumenter/production-forecasting-optimistic-and-overconfidentover-and-over-again.pdf); [Bond 2024](https://www.sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2024/02.06.-07.-making-good-decisions/presentations/1-2-marc-bond---bias-and-uncertainty.pdf)
- Context: 125 NCS fields approved 1972-2021; 94 producing in early 2022; RNB field forecasts and PDO forecasts are not public. - [Nesvold and Bratvold 2022](https://www.sodir.no/globalassets/2-force/2023/dokumenter/debiasing-production-forecasts_published-version.pdf)

**Reserve growth (Sodir resource accounts, 2025)**
- Resource Accounts as of 31.12.2025: NCS oil production in 2025 was the highest since 2009; resource growth replaced about 60% of production ("more than half"); total resources rose by 111 million Sm3 o.e. vs. the previous year (search extract; the category of the 111 figure was not confirmed). - [Sodir news 2026](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2026/high-production-and-increase-in-petroleum-resources/); [Resource accounts 2025](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-accounts/resource-accounts-2025/)
- A separate 2026 Sodir item is headlined "Production outpaces resource growth on the NCS". - [Sodir 2026](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2026/production-outpaces-resource-growth-on-the-ncs)

**Deferred production / uptime value**
- Offshore Norge (June 2026 lockout): estimated combined historical and future production loss of 2.4 million boe, valued at about NOK 1.6 billion (USD 164 million); losses about 12,000 boe/d vs. normal operations. Implied about NOK 670 per boe. - [Offshore Norge 2026](https://www.offshorenorge.no/en/about-us/news/2026/06/lockout-implemented-production-increasingly-affected)
- Offshore Norge shared spare-parts (sharing economy) project: estimated gain of more than NOK 1 billion from reduced production downtime (Jan 2021). - [Offshore Norge 2021](https://www.offshorenorge.no/en/about-us/news/2021/01/big-savings-with-sharing-economy-project/)
- Third-party research cited by Offshore Technology: asset production efficiency fell about 1% per year since 2004 to record lows on UKCS and NCS (older article, consultant source, undated in extract). - [Offshore Technology](https://www.offshore-technology.com/features/featurethe-economics-of-efficiency-offshore-maintenance-in-the-north-sea-4844363/)
- Aker BP: current production efficiency 96% (May 2026 Cognite release); AI agents for root-cause analysis cut engineering time by more than 70% (Sept 2025). - [Business Wire May 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260521650379/en/); [World Oil Sept 2025](https://www.worldoil.com/news/2025/9/16/aker-bp-expands-ai-first-strategy-with-cognite-to-transform-e-p-efficiency/)
- Equinor plans to hold NCS production around 1.2 million boe/d through 2035 (2020 level). - [Equinor Jan 2026](https://www.equinor.com/news/20260107-artificial-intelligence-saved-equinor-usd-130-million)

**Production optimisation with numbers**
- Johan Sverdrup (Equinor, 5 Oct 2020): digital solutions increased earnings by more than NOK 2 billion in the first year on stream, mainly from higher stable production via automated production optimisation; first-year output about 130 million barrels worth about NOK 50 billion at USD 40/boe; plateau raised from 440,000 to 470,000 (bbl/d); recovery ambition >70%. No per-tool breakdown published. - [Equinor](https://www.equinor.com/en/news/20201005-johan-sverdrup-first-year.html); [Sodir fact box, Resource report 2022](https://sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2022/3-competition-and-value-creation/fact-box--digital-solutions-on-johan-sverdrup)
- Solution Seeker (Norwegian data-driven production optimisation; ConocoPhillips and Engie co-developed from 2014): expected uplift 2-5% above historic best practice (GeoExpro); BCG white paper with Solution Seeker (Feb 2025) assumes typical 5-10% uplift (vendor-aligned). Vår Energi selected Solution Seeker for digital production optimisation (Nov 2023, "Vår Digital"). - [GeoExpro](https://geoexpro.com/artificial-intelligence-in-oil-gas-production/); [BCG PDF](https://media-publications.bcg.com/AI-Scale-in-Upstream-Oil-Gas-Production-Optimization.pdf); [Offshore magazine](https://www.offshore-mag.com/business-briefs/company-news/article/14298785/var-energi-picks-solution-seeker-for-digital-production-initiative)
- BP "Optimization Genie" (non-NCS, Gulf of America, Atlantis): about 2,000 bbl/d from optimised well settings at no extra cost, fully implemented by Sept 2024; BP says AI and analytics raised production by about 4% from 2022-2024. - [bp](https://www.bp.com/en/global/corporate/news-and-insights/energy-in-focus/top-digital-tech-driving-production.html)

### Inferences
- Value of 1% uptime (derived, not published): 1% of Equinor's ~1.2 million boe/d is ~12,000 boe/d; at the Offshore Norge-implied ~NOK 670/boe this is roughly NOK 2.9 billion/year for Equinor's NCS portfolio alone. Use as an order-of-magnitude anchor, clearly labelled as a back-of-envelope calculation.
- Johan Sverdrup's NOK 2 billion is about 4% of first-year revenue (2/50), consistent with the 2-5% uplift range claimed for data-driven optimisation.
- Hackathon angle with public data: Sodir FactPages publish monthly field production and PDO/field reserves history, so a "reference-class forecast" app (predict a new field's profile from analogue NCS fields' normalised curves, and show how optimistic an operator-style curve is) is buildable in 2 days and is directly backed by a Sodir-hosted FORCE paper. Field-level RNB forecasts are not public, so bias must be shown via reserve-estimate changes over time (FactPages "reserves" history) rather than forecast vs. actual.
- Water cut / intervention candidate selection and history matching need well-level rate and pressure data; NCS public data is mostly field-level monthly (well-level only for Volve), so these ideas are better demoed on Volve.

### Gaps
- No public Sodir/Havtil statistic on NCS-wide production efficiency or deferred production in NOK was found; the "value of 1% uptime" is derived above.
- Could not retrieve Sodir's own published forecast-accuracy analysis (e.g. RNB vs. actual at NCS level); norskpetroleum.no "Production forecasts" page exists but was not fetched. [norskpetroleum](https://www.norskpetroleum.no/en/production-and-exports/production-forecasts/)
- No sourced numbers found for ML water-cut prediction or well-intervention candidate selection value on the NCS, nor for IOR/EOR screening value 2020-2026.
- Aker BP has not published a NOK value for its Cognite programme (only efficiency and time-saving percentages).
- ConocoPhillips Ekofisk AI deployment with numbers: not found.

## 2. Exploration: success rates, discoveries, cost and value of better risking

### Takeaway
NCS technical success rates are high (~50% for wildcats 2010-2024; 21 discoveries from 40 wildcats in 2025, the best year in a decade excluding 2021), but discoveries are small (67 million Sm3 o.e. in total for 2025's 21 finds), so value lies less in "will it find hydrocarbons" and more in "is it big enough and close enough to develop". Sodir estimates exploration 2004-2023 created more than NOK 2,000 billion NPV on about NOK 660 billion spent.

### Cited Findings
- 2025: 49 exploration wells completed (40 wildcat, 9 appraisal; 29 North Sea, 12 Norwegian Sea, 6 Barents Sea), seven more than 2024; 21 discoveries (11 North Sea, 5 Norwegian Sea, 5 Barents Sea), five of them in development wells with exploration targets; preliminary 67 million Sm3 o.e. recoverable, about twice as much liquid as gas; "slightly fewer wells are expected in 2026" (page updated 28.05.2026). - [norskpetroleum.no](https://www.norskpetroleum.no/en/exploration/exploration-activity/)
- 2010-2024: 468 wildcat wells completed, 229 discoveries, about 50% success, "high by international standards". - [norskpetroleum.no](https://www.norskpetroleum.no/en/exploration/exploration-activity/)
- Sodir Resource Report 2026 (search extract): 2025 was the best exploration year in ten years except 2021; 40 exploration wells, 21 discoveries. Note: Sodir counts 40 (wildcats) vs. norskpetroleum 49 (incl. appraisal). - [Sodir RR2026 exploration](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2026/exploration/); [Sodir news](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2026/best-exploration-year-in-several-years/)
- Undiscovered resources: about 3.5 billion Sm3 o.e. (18% North Sea, 21% Norwegian Sea, 61% Barents Sea). - [norskpetroleum.no](https://www.norskpetroleum.no/en/exploration/exploration-activity/)
- Value of exploration (Sodir, 2024 analysis of 2004-2023): about NOK 660 billion invested; total net cash flow about NOK 3,800 billion; NPV more than NOK 2,000 billion; profitable in all ocean areas; value of discoveries more than twice exploration costs over the last five years (search extract). - [Sodir 2024](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2024/exploration-activity-has-been-profitable-in-all-ocean-areas)
- Earlier Sodir analysis (2020): net present value about NOK 1,700 billion at 4% discount and NOK 1,200 billion at 7%; Barents Sea profitable but lower value creation; costs include dry wells. - [Sodir 2020](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2020/profitable-exploration-on-the-norwegian-shelf)
- Sodir has a public exploration dashboard tracking activity and a "technical discovery rate". - [Sodir 2025 dashboard news](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2025/new-dashboard-provides-insight-into-exploration-activity-on-the-ncs/); [exploration well statistics](https://www.sodir.no/en/facts/data-and-analyses/exploration-wells-statistics/)
- Global comparison: Westwood reports 2025 high-impact wells worldwide delivered 4.8 bn boe at a 31% commercial success rate (not comparable to Sodir's technical rate). - [Westwood](https://www.westwoodenergy.com/news/westwood-insight-the-state-of-exploration-2025)
- APA 2025 (announced 13 Jan 2026): 57 new production licences to 19 companies (31 North Sea, 21 Norwegian Sea, 5 Barents Sea); 13 companies offered operatorships; Equinor 35 licences (17 operated), Aker BP 22 (12 operated), DNO 17 (4 operated), OMV 4. Applicant count differs between sources (19-21). - [Regjeringen](https://www.regjeringen.no/en/whats-new/57-new-production-licenses-awarded/id3145952); [Sodir APA 2025](https://www.sodir.no/en/facts/production-licences/licensing-rounds/apa-2025); [Aker BP](https://live.euronext.com/en/products/equities/company-news/2026-01-13-aker-bp-awarded-22-licenses-apa-2025)
- Equinor reports AI interpreted 2 million km2 of seismic in 2025 with a tenfold capacity increase (no USD value stated). - [Equinor Jan 2026](https://www.equinor.com/news/20260107-artificial-intelligence-saved-equinor-usd-130-million)

### Inferences
- Average 2025 discovery size is about 3.2 million Sm3 o.e. (67/21), i.e. ~20 million boe; this is the "small discovery" regime where development economics, not geological success, is the binding constraint. That shifts hackathon value toward commerciality and analog screening rather than pure chance-of-success prediction.
- An "analog retrieval" app (given a prospect or new discovery, retrieve the most similar NCS discoveries/fields from FactPages by area, play/formation, depth, fluid, size, and show what happened to them: developed, tied back, or "development unlikely") is directly supported by public FactPages wellbore, discovery and field tables, and pairs naturally with an LLM to write a risked summary.
- APA awards (57 in Jan 2026) plus the annual APA cycle create a recurring screening need: an app that ranks APA blocks by nearby discoveries, dry wells and infrastructure is relevant to every licence applicant.

### Gaps
- No sourced figure for the cost of a typical NCS dry well (commonly quoted NOK several hundred million per well, but no 2020-2026 primary source found in this session).
- No published quantification of the value of better prospect risking on the NCS (e.g. NOK saved per avoided dry well) was found.
- Annual NCS exploration cost in NOK for 2024-2025 is shown in a norskpetroleum chart but the numbers were not in the page text.
- APA 2026 round (deadline typically September 2026, awards typically January) status was not checked.

## 3. Near-field exploration, undeveloped discoveries and tie-back screening

### Takeaway
At end-2025 Sodir counts about 91 discoveries awaiting a development decision with over 500 million Sm3 o.e. of contingent resources; most are small and need tie-back to existing hosts, and the window closes as hosts mature. This is the clearest public-data, high-value problem for a hackathon, but no Sodir NOK value for "better tie-back matching" was found.

### Cited Findings
- End-2025: 91 discoveries awaiting a possible development decision; Sodir RR2026 summary: "over 90 discoveries" with over 500 million scm o.e. contingent resources; most are in the North Sea; because they are small, profitable development largely requires tie-back to existing infrastructure, as subsea developments or wells from existing facilities (search extract). - [Sodir RR2026 summary](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2026/summary/); [Resource accounts 2025](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-accounts/resource-accounts-2025/resource-accounts/)
- End-2024 comparison: 78 discoveries totalling 472 million Sm3 (roughly half liquids, half gas); 11 discoveries moved to "development unlikely" (search extract). Largest undeveloped: Wisting (7324/8-1, Barents), Linnorm (6406/9-1, Norwegian Sea), Peon (35/2-1, North Sea). - [Sodir resource accounts 2024](https://sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-accounts/resource-accounts-2024/discovered-resources); [The Shelf in 2024](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/the-shelf/the-shelf-in-2024/?print=2)
- Resource Report 2024: 82 gas discoveries not yet developed, of which extraction is highly unlikely for 47 (search extract). - [Sodir RR2024 PDF](https://sodir.no/globalassets/1-sodir/publikasjoner/rapporter/ressursrapporter/2024/en/resource-report-2024.pdf)
- Sodir: development of many discoveries depends on capacity in nearby host facilities; wells drilled from existing facilities can make very small finds profitable if close to other discoveries or fields. - [Sodir RR2017](https://sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2017/recovery/develop-all-profitable-discoveries)
- Sodir FORCE 2026 event "Unlocking the potential": the economic window for tie-ins narrows as host fields mature and production costs rise; decisions must balance material uncertainty with time-critical tie-in opportunities. - [FORCE abstract](https://www.sodir.no/en/force/events/in-person-unlocking-the-potential/abstract)
- Area solutions: Sodir's Trell and Trine fact box says coordinated area development reduces total development cost vs. developing each discovery separately; Norne FPSO extended beyond 2025 (twice the original plan) through nearby tie-backs. - [Trell and Trine](https://sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2022/3-competition-and-value-creation/fact-box--trell-and-trine); [Norne area](https://sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2020/4-Significance-of-exploration/Fact-box-4-4-Exploring-near-infrastructure-and-extended-production-the-Norne-area)
- Barents Sea: only two producing fields and lagging infrastructure; more infrastructure could open small discoveries. - [Sodir RR2020 fact box 4.3](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/publications/reports/resource-report/resource-report-2020/4-Significance-of-exploration/Fact-box-4-3-Infrastructure-development-and-increase-profitability-in-the-Barents-Sea)
- Sodir FactPages expose per-discovery descriptions, status and resource estimates, and per-field data. - [FactPages discovery table](https://factpages.sodir.no/en/discovery/TableView/Description)
- Tight reservoirs: 2026 Sodir item on profitable development of tight reservoirs (not fetched). - [Sodir 2026](https://www.sodir.no/en/whats-new/news/general-news/2026/profitable-development-of-tight-reservoirs/)

### Inferences
- Order-of-magnitude value of the undeveloped portfolio: 500 million Sm3 o.e. is ~3.1 billion boe; at the Offshore Norge-implied ~NOK 670/boe that is ~NOK 2,100 billion gross revenue at stake (not NPV; derived, not sourced).
- A "tie-back matchmaker" app is fully buildable from public data: FactPages discoveries (location, status, volumes, fluid) + fields/facilities + pipelines (Sodir map services) + field production profiles (declining host production as a proxy for spare ullage and remaining host life). Rank each undeveloped discovery by distance to hosts, host decline (capacity freeing up), fluid compatibility, and host cessation risk; LLM writes a one-page screening memo. This aligns with Sodir's stated "time-critical" framing.
- Combining 2 and 3: "near-field exploration radar" that flags APA blocks and prospects within tie-back distance of hosts with declining production.

### Gaps
- Sodir does not publish host spare capacity (ullage) directly; it must be proxied from production decline vs. historical peak.
- No sourced NOK value for "time-critical tie-back" losses (value lost when a host ceases before a nearby discovery is developed).
- Exact per-discovery 2025 volumes were not retrieved (available in Sodir Resource Accounts Excel and FactPages).

## 5. Research frontier: surrogates, foundation models and LLM agents for reservoir engineering (2024-2026)

### Takeaway
The live research direction is LLM agents that drive existing simulators or build surrogates (SINTEF JutulGPT, AutoSurrogate 2026), not a single reservoir foundation model; transformer production-forecasting papers exist but are benchmark-scale and not NCS-validated.

### Cited Findings
- SINTEF JutulGPT (blog 27 Mar 2026, edited 19 Jun 2026; work started summer 2025): natural-language agent over the open-source JutulDarcy simulator with an interpret-act-validate loop, RAG over docstrings/source/examples, lint-then-run with self-repair; ran a 300,000-cell, 10-year heterogeneous anticline case; key lessons: one-shot generation performs poorly, iteration plus linting helps substantially, silent simulator defaults (e.g. compressibility) cause divergent models; does not yet do history matching, UQ or optimisation; no formal benchmark. Authors say they would now use Claude Code with JutulDarcy exposed as an MCP server. - [SINTEF blog](https://blog.sintef.com/digital-en/talking-to-your-simulator-what-we-learned-building-jutulgpt/)
- AutoSurrogate (Liu and Wang, arXiv 2604.11945, 13 Apr 2026): four LLM agents do data profiling, architecture selection, Bayesian hyperparameter optimisation, training and QA to build deep-learning surrogates for subsurface flow from a one-sentence request; demo maps permeability to pressure and CO2 saturation over 31 timesteps in a 3D CO2 storage model; claims to beat expert baselines and AutoML (no numbers in abstract). - [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11945)
- EAGE 2026 paper on agent-assisted construction of differentiable conceptual reservoir models from natural-language descriptions (cites arXiv 2603.00214 on agentic scientific simulation, not opened). - [EarthDoc](https://www.earthdoc.org/content/papers/10.3997/2214-4609.202637165)
- In-context-learning LLM for reservoir management tasks validated against numerical simulation on a benchmark model (Arabian Journal for Science and Engineering, Dec 2025). - [KFUPM](https://pure.kfupm.edu.sa/en/publications/enhancing-large-language-models-with-in-context-learning-for-rese/)

### Inferences
- For a 2-day hackathon, the agent pattern that SINTEF validated (LLM + RAG over docs + tool execution + validation loop) maps directly onto "LLM agent over Sodir FactPages + FORCE/Sodir reports", which is lower risk than training a surrogate.
- Given the Nesvold and Bratvold result that simple reference-class forecasts beat complex ones, a transformer forecasting model is unlikely to be the strongest pitch on NCS field-level monthly data; an analog/reference-class approach with an LLM explanation layer is both cheaper and better supported by NCS evidence.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 arXiv paper found on a pretrained "foundation model" for reservoir simulation or for NCS production time series; a targeted search (e.g. "time series foundation model oil production", "neural operator reservoir foundation") is needed.
- Quantitative speedups/accuracies for 2024-2026 ML proxies for history matching were not collected in this session.
