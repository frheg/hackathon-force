Barrier Ledger

Legacy wells around a CO₂ store, or any area, laid out for a plugging or storage decision: do they cross the seal and the reservoir, is casing across the seal, when and how were they abandoned. Three published-style screening schemes run side by side, and the app shows where they disagree and which missing fact causes it. Click a well for its barrier picture, extracted from its history and reports with citations.
