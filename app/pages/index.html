Three sketches of an oil and gas app

FORCE VibeCode Hackathon, Stavanger, 19 to 20 November 2026

NCS Copilot

Ask about the Norwegian shelf in plain words. The model writes SQL over Sodir's FactPages, the server runs it read-only, and you get the answer, the table, the query and a map of the wells.

Data: Sodir FactPages (fields, wellbores, discoveries, licences, monthly production, reserves).

Open NCS Copilot

Log Lens

Well logs next to the lithology, as interpreted and as predicted by a model trained on 98 other wells. Zoom to an interval and the model explains it like a petrophysicist.

Data: FORCE 2020 lithology competition (CC BY 4.0), shown on the 10 wells held out from training.

Open Log Lens

Field Pulse

A field's monthly production, a decline curve fitted from a month you pick, and its forecast against Sodir's remaining reserves. The model writes a field brief from the numbers and Sodir's description.

Data: Sodir FactPages monthly production, reserves and field descriptions.

Open Field Pulse

Built on

Ideas from earlier work: the FORCE 2020 lithology competition (329 teams; Log Lens uses its data and score), the FORCE ML hackathon projects, and text-to-SQL assistants for well data such as SLB's drilling work and bpx energy's Xpert. The model runs locally; connection status.
